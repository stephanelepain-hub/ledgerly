Privacy Policy for Ledgerly

Effective date: 24 July 2026

Ledgerly (“the app,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is a personal expense and receipt-recording application. This Privacy Policy explains how Ledgerly handles information when you use the app.

Summary. Ledgerly is designed to keep your financial records on your device. It does not require an account to create and manage transactions. It does not use advertising, behavioural advertising, analytics tracking, or sell your personal information.

Information handled by the app

Transaction and financial-record information

When you create or edit a transaction, Ledgerly may process information you enter or confirm, including transaction amount, date, merchant name, category, description, notes, line items, tax information, and receipt-derived information.

This information is stored locally in Ledgerly’s on-device database. It is not uploaded to Ledgerly’s servers as part of ordinary app use.

Receipt images and camera access

Ledgerly requests camera access only when you choose to scan a receipt. It may also request access to photos or media when you choose an existing receipt image from your device.

Receipt images are used to display and process the receipt in the app. Confirmed receipt images are stored in Ledgerly’s app-specific storage on your device. We do not upload receipt images to our servers.

You can deny or revoke camera and photo/media permission at any time through your device settings. The receipt-scanning features will not work without the relevant permission.

On-device text recognition

Ledgerly uses on-device text recognition to read receipt text and suggest transaction details. This processing is performed locally on your device.

Optional cloud receipt-text retry

If on-device receipt recognition has low confidence, Ledgerly may offer an optional cloud-processing retry. This feature is used only if you explicitly select “Send text” after seeing the in-app confirmation.

If you choose this option, Ledgerly sends the recognized receipt text to the configured cloud-processing service to return suggested transaction details. Ledgerly does not send the receipt image through this feature, and your locally stored ledger and receipt images remain on your device.

Receipt text may contain purchase, merchant, date, amount, or other information visible on the receipt. Do not use the optional cloud retry if you do not want that recognized text transmitted for processing.

Data stored on your device

Ledgerly stores the following locally on your device:

  • your transactions, categories, and app settings;
  • receipt images you choose to save with transactions;
  • temporary receipt-processing information; and
  • a local text-only OCR diagnostic file used for troubleshooting, if generated. This diagnostic does not include receipt images.

You can delete individual transactions from the app. Deleting a transaction also attempts to delete Ledgerly’s app-owned saved receipt image for that transaction. Uninstalling Ledgerly removes app data from the device, subject to your device’s operating-system and backup settings.

Data sharing

Ledgerly does not sell, rent, or share your personal information for advertising or marketing.

The app transmits recognized receipt text only when you voluntarily use the optional cloud receipt-text retry described above. Ledgerly may also use your device’s standard sharing interface when you expressly choose to export or share a report; the destination and recipients are selected by you.

No advertising or tracking

Ledgerly does not contain third-party advertising and does not use tracking technologies to build advertising profiles or track you across apps or websites.

Children’s privacy

Ledgerly is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

Ledgerly is designed to store your ledger data locally on your device. You are responsible for protecting access to your device, including by using device-level security features such as a screen lock.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. If you choose the optional cloud receipt-text retry or export/share data using your device, the handling of that data may also be subject to the policies of the relevant service or recipient.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Ledgerly’s data practices change. We will publish the updated policy at this public URL and update the effective date.
